About EduVille

Built by students, for students

EduVille is a free education platform, created in Zimbabwe, for every African student with a dream to learn.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide every African student with free, high-quality education — no matter where they live, what school they attend, or how much money they have.

"Commit to Your Future."

📖 Our Story

EduVille was born in Chitungwiza, Zimbabwe, in 2025 — from an idea that started with one student asking a simple question:

"What if there was a GitHub for education — a place where students could share notes, past papers, and tutorials freely?"

That student was Edmore Taroiwa, then in Form 5 at Seke 4 High School. He imagined a platform where African students could access the same quality of study material regardless of their background.

Together with his friend Benjamin Nyamukusa, he started building EduVille. First on Termux, then on GitHub Pages with Firebase. The platform grew — with video lessons, study notes, past papers, an AI tutor, and progress tracking.

Today, EduVille serves students across Zimbabwe, Botswana, and beyond — and it remains completely free.

❤️ Why We Do This

We started EduVille because we know what it's like to study without proper resources.

We know the struggle of finding quality notes, the cost of past paper books, the frustration of not understanding a topic and having no one to ask.

EduVille is our answer to that. It's a platform we wish we'd had when we started Form 1.

If even one student passes their exams because of EduVille, the work is worth it.
