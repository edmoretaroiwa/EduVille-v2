About EduVille

Built by students, for students

A free education platform, created in Zimbabwe, for every African student with a dream to learn.

🎯 Our mission

To give every African student free, high-quality education — no matter where they live, what school they attend, or how much money they have.

"Commit to Your Future."

📖 Our story

EduVille was born in Chitungwiza, Zimbabwe, in 2025 — from an idea that started with one student asking a simple question:

"What if there was a place where students could share notes, past papers and tutorials freely?"

That student was Edmore Taroiwa, then in Form 5 at Seke 4 High School. He imagined a platform where African students could access the same quality of study material regardless of their background.

Together with his friend Benjamin Nyamukusa, he started building EduVille. First on Termux, then on GitHub Pages. The platform grew — with video lessons, study notes, past paper references, an AI tutor, and progress tracking.

Today, EduVille serves students across Zimbabwe, Botswana and beyond — and it remains completely free.

I'm Edmore Taroiwa, born 28 November 2007 in Harare.

I spent Grades 1–7 in Sanyati village at Sanyati Baptist Primary School. I passed my Grade 7 exams, but I didn't get into the high school I hoped for — for reasons I still don't fully understand.

In primary, I had my crew: Leon, Agrippa, Tadiwa and Tanatswa. We did everything together. But life moved us in different directions.

I moved to Chitungwiza and had to start over. New place, new accent to be laughed at. I got over it.

In Form 3, I found my brothers: Dioney, Elvis and Benjamin. We called ourselves the League of Legends, from League of Nations. We all passed Form 4 together. Dion and Elvis went to polytechnic, Benjamin and I went to A-Level — but we were never separated.

I've always been the ambitious kid — coding, making beats. I started making beats on my phone with FL Studio Mobile in Form 3, and got better with time. In Form 5, I started learning to code — on Termux, with Python, with a lot of help from AI.

Today I'm building a free online learning platform: EduVille. It's not perfect yet. But for an African kid with just a simple Android phone, I'm doing my best. I'm learning while I build.

I used to think I needed fancy gadgets. I didn't. I needed determination, consistency and hard work.

And I'm committed to helping every African learner through EduVille.

🛠️ How we work

We know that trust matters. Here's exactly how EduVille operates:

  • Curriculum-aligned content. Every lesson and video is matched to the ZIMSEC, Cambridge, or BEC syllabus it belongs to.
  • Teacher-created and reviewed. Content is written by practising educators, or submitted by teachers and checked before it goes live.
  • No copyrighted material hosted. We do not host paid or copyrighted textbooks, exam papers, or course material without permission. Where we can, we link to official sources.
  • Original practice questions. Our practice and revision content is written by our team — not copied from anywhere.
  • Content corrections welcome. If you spot a mistake, tell us. We fix it and credit you. Email ttaroiwa@gmail.com.

🤖 Our AI policy

TymerCRE, our AI study tutor, is a study assistant — not an authority. We want students to understand its limits:

  • AI can make mistakes. Always verify important answers with your teacher, textbook, or an official marking scheme.
  • Powered by Google Gemini. Your questions are processed by Google's AI service. We do not use your chats for advertising.
  • No personal data required. You can use the AI tutor without giving your name or email.
  • Anonymous storage. Chat logs are stored anonymously to improve the tutor and for safety review.

🆓 Our promise to students

EduVille will always be free for students. Not "free with ads." Not "free until graduation." Not "free — sign up to unlock."

Free. Period.

We fund the platform ourselves, through our own time and, in the future, through partnerships with organisations that share our mission — never by charging students or selling their data.

If EduVille ever has to change that, we'll tell you publicly, on this page, before it happens. That's our commitment.

❤️ Why we do this

We started EduVille because we know what it's like to study without proper resources.

We know the struggle of finding quality notes, the cost of textbooks, the frustration of not understanding a topic and having no one to ask.

EduVille is our answer to that. It's the platform we wish we'd had when we started Form 1.

If even one student passes their exams because of EduVille, the work is worth it.
