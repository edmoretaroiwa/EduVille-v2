What is TymerCRE?

TymerCRE is EduVille's AI study tutor. It's a chat assistant that helps you understand topics from your ZIMSEC, Cambridge, or BEC syllabus. You type a question, and it explains the answer in simple English — the same way a patient teacher would.

It was built specifically for EduVille students. It's not a general-purpose chatbot; it's focused on school subjects and exam preparation.

How it works

Ask a question the way you'd ask a teacher. Here's what a typical chat looks like:

You
Explain quadratic equations
TymerCRE
A quadratic equation has the form ax² + bx + c = 0.

To solve it, you have three main methods:
1. Factorise — works when the numbers are simple
2. Complete the square — useful for finding the vertex
3. Quadratic formula — always works:
   x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a
You
Can you solve 2x² + 5x − 3 = 0?
TymerCRE
Yes! Here, a = 2, b = 5, c = −3.

Using the formula:
x = (−5 ± √(25 − 4·2·(−3))) / 4
x = (−5 ± √49) / 4
x = (−5 ± 7) / 4

So x = 0.5 or x = −3.

You can ask follow-up questions, request simpler explanations, or ask for more examples. TymerCRE remembers the conversation within a session so you don't have to repeat yourself.

What it can't do (and why that matters)

TymerCRE is a study assistant, not an unquestionable authority. Like all AI tools, it can sometimes:

  • Give a wrong answer confidently
  • Misunderstand a question
  • Miss exam-specific conventions or marking-scheme requirements

⚠️ Always verify important answers with your teacher, your textbook, or an official marking scheme. Use TymerCRE to help you understand, not to replace your own working.

Your data and privacy

We take student privacy seriously. Here's what you should know about how TymerCRE handles your information:

  • No personal data required — you can use TymerCRE without giving your name or email.
  • Chats are stored anonymously — for improving the tutor and for safety review. They aren't linked to any personal profile.
  • Your questions go to Google's Gemini API — the AI that powers TymerCRE. Their privacy policy applies to the AI processing.
  • We never sell your data — to anyone, for any reason.

Frequently asked questions

Is TymerCRE really free?

Yes. Completely free, with no limits and no ads. We may add paid features in the future, but the core AI tutor will always be free for students.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. You can start chatting right away without signing up. If you create an account, we'll be able to remember your progress on other EduVille features.

Which subjects does it support best?

Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computer Studies. It can also help with exam technique and study skills.

Is it ChatGPT?

No. TymerCRE is built specifically for EduVille using Google's Gemini AI. It's tuned to explain concepts at a secondary-school level and focused on ZIMSEC, Cambridge, and BEC content.

Can it write my essays for me?

It can help you structure ideas and understand how to approach a question, but it won't do your homework for you — and it shouldn't. The goal is to help you learn, not to replace your own work.

What if it gives a wrong answer?

Check with your teacher, textbook, or an official marking scheme. If you spot something repeatedly wrong, please let us know — we're constantly improving TymerCRE. Report issues at ttaroiwa@gmail.com.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. TymerCRE works on any phone with a web browser. It's lightweight and doesn't require a fast connection.
