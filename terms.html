The rules for using EduVille

Last updated: 27 September 2026

1. Welcome to EduVille

EduVille is a free educational platform built to help African students learn, share knowledge, and prepare for their exams. By using EduVille, you agree to these Terms of Service. Please read them carefully.

2. Who Can Use EduVille

EduVille is free and open to:

  • Students — anyone studying ZIMSEC, Cambridge, BEC, or similar curricula
  • Teachers — educators who want to share lessons, notes, and resources
  • Self-learners — anyone interested in learning independently

Users under 13 years old should have a parent or guardian's permission before creating an account.

3. Your Account

When you create an EduVille account, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate information (real name and email)
  • Keep your password secure and private
  • Not share your account with others
  • Notify us immediately if your account is compromised

You are responsible for all activity that happens under your account.

4. Acceptable Use

You agree NOT to:

  • Upload any content that is illegal, harmful, or offensive
  • Post copyrighted material you do not own or have permission to share
  • Harass, bully, or threaten other users
  • Attempt to hack, spam, or disrupt EduVille
  • Use EduVille for any commercial purpose without permission
  • Impersonate another person or organization
  • Share false or misleading educational content

Violation of these rules may result in account suspension or permanent removal.

5. Content You Upload

When you upload videos, notes, or past papers to EduVille:

  • You keep ownership of your content
  • You give EduVille permission to display it on the platform
  • You confirm that you have the right to share it
  • You understand that other users can view and learn from it
  • You agree not to upload material that infringes on someone else's copyright

EduVille reserves the right to remove any content that violates these terms.

6. Educational Content Disclaimer

EduVille provides educational content for learning purposes only. While we work hard to ensure accuracy:

  • Lessons, notes, and videos are created by teachers and users
  • Our AI tutor (TymerCRE) may occasionally make mistakes
  • Content should be used alongside official textbooks and teacher guidance
  • Always verify important information with your teachers or official sources

EduVille is not responsible for outcomes related to your exams or academic performance.

EduVille respects the copyright of exam boards, publishers, and creators.

If you own content on EduVille and want it removed, please contact us at ttaroiwa@gmail.com with:
